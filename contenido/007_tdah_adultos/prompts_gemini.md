Adjunta la referencia de El Observador (y la de La Observadora si se indica).

```
Create a VERTICAL 9:16 video, 8 seconds, 24 fps.
CHARACTER: "The Observer" exactly as in the attached reference — warm white soft body, thin soft charcoal outline, round head separate from a pear-shaped body, two small SEPARATE dot eyes, short eyebrows, tiny mouth, long soft arms. Never connect the eyes with a line. Same proportions in every frame.
STYLE: flat 2D hand-drawn illustration, simple flat background in the same 2D style, NOT photographic, NOT 3D. No sepia or yellow tint.
CAMERA: static, no zoom, no cuts. Show the full body; the character is medium-small, centered. Leave the top third empty.
NO text, NO letters, NO audio.
SCENE: Simple flat 2D desk with a laptop and a wall calendar. The Observer sits at the desk.
ACTION: 0–3 s it scrolls on the phone, relaxed. 3–5 s it looks at the calendar and its eyes widen. 5–8 s it types extremely fast on the laptop, focused, small speed lines around its hands.
```
