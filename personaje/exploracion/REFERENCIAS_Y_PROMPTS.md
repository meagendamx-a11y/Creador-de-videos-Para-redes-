## 3. Prompts para otras IAs

Funcionan mejor en **inglés**. Sirven en ChatGPT (imagen), Gemini (Nano Banana), Midjourney, Leonardo, Ideogram.
No uses “in the style of [artista]”: describe los rasgos (evita problemas legales y éticos).

### 3.1 Plantilla maestra (exploración)

```
Original mascot character design for a psychology education channel.
CHARACTER: {DESCRIPCIÓN DEL CONCEPTO}
PERSONALITY: gentle, introspective, a bit shy, relatable, kind.
DESIGN RULES: very simple silhouette readable at thumbnail size, rounded shapes with no sharp corners,
small dot eyes with expressive eyebrows, tiny or no mouth, short noodle arms and stubby legs,
no clothes, no text, no specific age, gender or ethnicity.
STYLE: {ESTILO}
COLOR: {PALETA}
Full body, front view, centered, plain off-white background, soft contact shadow.
```

### 3.2 Bloques {DESCRIPCIÓN DEL CONCEPTO}

- **Ajolote:** `a small chubby axolotl standing upright, round wide head, three soft frilly gills on each side of the head that droop when sad and lift when happy, short tail, wide gentle smile line`
- **Brote:** `a small egg-shaped seed creature with a tiny green two-leaf sprout growing from the top of its head; the sprout droops when it is sad and blooms a small flower when it is happy`
- **Nube:** `a small fluffy cloud creature with tiny arms, floating slightly above the ground; it turns grey and drizzles when sad, a small sun peeks out when happy`
- **Gota:** `a small teardrop-shaped water creature with a soft highlight, tiny arms and legs`
- **Persona simple:** `a minimalist rounded human figure with a big round head and soft bell-shaped body, single hair curl, non-realistic sage-green skin`

### 3.3 Bloques {ESTILO}

- **Vector limpio:** `clean flat vector illustration, uniform dark charcoal outline, flat colors, subtle highlight, modern sticker style`
- **Trazo a mano:** `hand-drawn marker linework with slightly wobbly imperfect lines, flat colors, warm and friendly, children's picture book meets modern editorial`
- **Acuarela / cuento:** `loose ink line with soft watercolor washes, paper texture, gentle storybook illustration, muted warm palette`
- **Kawaii pastel:** `soft pastel kawaii style, thin warm-brown outline, tiny dot eyes, blush cheeks, cozy and calming`
- **Editorial con grano:** `editorial illustration with risograph grain texture, muted earthy palette, slight print misregistration, sophisticated but warm`

### 3.4 Bloques {PALETA}

- Cálida: `soft pink, warm cream, charcoal outline, accents of sage green`
- Calma: `muted teal, sand beige, dusty lavender, charcoal outline`

### 3.5 Hoja de personaje (cuando ya elijas uno)

```
Character turnaround and expression sheet of the SAME character, consistent proportions and colors:
row 1: front view, 3/4 view, side view, back view;
row 2: expressions — neutral, sad, happy, anxious, thinking, angry, surprised, calm/relieved;
row 3: poses — sitting hugging knees, walking, waving, holding a cup of tea, lying in bed awake at night.
{DESCRIPCIÓN DEL CONCEPTO} {ESTILO} {PALETA}
Plain off-white background, evenly spaced, no text labels.
```

### 3.6 Escena (para videos)

```
Using the attached character reference (keep the exact same design, proportions and colors),
illustrate: {ESCENA, ej. "the character sits alone at a desk late at night, phone glowing, surrounded by floating notification bubbles, feeling overwhelmed"}.
Vertical 9:16 composition, character occupies the lower-middle third, leave empty space at the top for captions.
{ESTILO}
```

### Consejos de consistencia

1. Genera muchas variantes de exploración y elige **una imagen “canon”**.
2. En las siguientes generaciones **adjunta esa imagen** como referencia (ChatGPT/Gemini: subir imagen; Midjourney: `--cref` / referencia de personaje; Leonardo: Character Reference).
3. Repite siempre los mismos bloques de texto.
4. Para animar gratis, conviene que yo **redibuje en vector** el diseño ganador: así queda 100% consistente y animable por código.
