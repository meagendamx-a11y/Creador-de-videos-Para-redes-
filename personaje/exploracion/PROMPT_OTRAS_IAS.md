# Prompt para generar opciones de personaje en otras IAs

Úsalo en ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney, etc. Pídelo 3–4 veces para tener más opciones.
Cuando una guste: *"develop #N further: 6 variations of the same concept"*.

```
I'm creating an ORIGINAL mascot character for a Spanish-language psychology education channel (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) aimed at young adults in Mexico. The character will narrate everyday human behavior ("the psychology of people who...") in short animated videos. It is a fictional guide, NOT a doctor or therapist.

What I need: a sheet with 12 DIFFERENT character concepts, numbered 1–12, each in a single simple front-facing pose, full body, on a plain off-white background. Keep them simple — flat colors, clean outline, minimal detail — like early concept sketches, so I can compare silhouettes and pick favorites.

Requirements for every concept:
- Must read as "someone" (a person or a human-like being), not an animal or object.
- Very simple, iconic silhouette recognizable at thumbnail size.
- No specific age, gender or ethnicity; non-realistic skin color is welcome.
- Expressive with very few elements (dot eyes, eyebrows, posture, long arms...).
- Calm, warm, slightly introspective personality; relatable, a bit awkward.
- Easy to animate in 2D (simple shapes), consistent across scenes.
- Avoid: generic 3D/Pixar look, anime, Ghibli style, sepia tint, realistic hands, clutter.
- Do not copy existing characters (no green elongated faceless figure, no blue teardrop aliens, no white round head with simple body, no brain with a face, no sprout on the head).

Explore a wide range across the 12: elongated faceless figures, bean-shaped humanoids, big-head small-body, geometric editorial people, hooded figures, faceless body-language figures, one-line minimal drawings, webcomic-style messy-haired person, shadow figure with eyes, smooth human-like beings, characters with a signature accessory (glasses, scarf, hood, hair curl).
```
